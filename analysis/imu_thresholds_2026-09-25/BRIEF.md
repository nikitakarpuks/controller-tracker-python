## Non-negotiable lessons (each cost this project real time)
* ALWAYS compose the mocap bridge (GT_vision = GT_rel o bridge^-1) before comparing to vision/filter poses; a raw comparison shows a constant ~178 deg/~80 mm "error". Sanity-check any new comparison on a known-good frame first.
