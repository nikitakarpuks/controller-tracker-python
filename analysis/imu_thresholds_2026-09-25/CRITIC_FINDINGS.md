# Critic findings to be resolved in the final spec (coordinator's condensed copy of C1 physics + C2 statistics reports)
## C1 (physics/frames) - verdict: A1/A2 numbers reproducible; frames/bridge/D-flip/lag/mocap timing correct (16/16 A1 rows and 10/10 A2 rows re-derived by own scripts, 0.00 deg diff). No hidden 178 deg/80 mm bias.
- BLOCKER: constant 45 deg rot veto unsafe. Cause: GYRO SATURATION. Raw sensor axes clip at ~+-2000 dps (x, ICM-20602 FS_SEL=3), ~2720 dps (z), y unresolved; walk_hard/static_hard bursts under-integrate by 30-45 deg; the 5 GOOD candidates with innov 29.9-41.9 deg (walk_hard right frames 1091,717,718,4873,4874; ~3 independent bursts) are gyro under-reading (e.g. 4873 predicted change 61.9 vs true 99.95 deg). peak_gyro is itself clipped => under-states exactly when it matters (recorded 3476 dps = norm of clipped components). Saturation scan (coordinator, analysis/imu_thresholds_2026-09-25/saturation/): only static_hard and walk_hard reach the ceilings (0.15-1.04 % of samples, 9-39 bursts per controller); other 6 recordings zero.
- MAJOR: the 0.05*G*dt gyro term is a fit (3 saturation events), not physics; GOOD innovation does not scale with G*dt (median 1.6-3.1 deg in every path bin). It is unbounded (thr up to 280 deg -> veto fails open again for state age > ~0.8 s).
- MAJOR closed loop (offline surrogate, 18 origins, frozen state coasted by gyro, headset imu0): BAD followers vetoed 83/128 (60/100 dedup), GOOD followers wrongly vetoed 0/1143 uncapped; unvetoed BAD followers are at state age 0.8-2.7 s (thr 85-280). Capping thr at 45-90 deg raises vetoed BAD to 73-91/100 BUT vetoes 10 GOOD followers of ONE event (static_hard right 5873, state age 1.8-2.2 s, 1880 dps burst; blind gyro coast through a burst for ~2 s is 95 deg off). => veto needs a state-age expiry (~1.0-1.5 s, unverified) or confirm-twice-and-release path. GOOD innovation vs frozen coast by state age: 1.0-1.5 s max 19.2 deg; 1.5-3 s max 98 deg.
- Headset ego-motion: MITIGATED for rotation. Replacing mocap headset ego-rotation with integrated headset imu0 gyro changes innovation by median 0.00, p90 0.70, p99 1.67, max 11.3 deg; R1 0 FP/1429 (dt>=50ms) and 240/313 catches identical => a DEPLOYABLE rotation source exists (imu0 gyro), but predict() does not use it (no headset-IMU ego-motion in code). Headset mocap has no drift term (imu0 vs headset mocap drifts -0.8 -> +4.1 ms across thirds): minor.
- State proxy (One-Euro reported pose vs raw accepted vision): mean +0.07 deg, p90 +0.42: essentially the vision pose (slightly pessimistic). Live-vs-replay innovation discrepancy n=3, mixed sign.
- Physical: IMU 200 Hz (5.0 ms), gaps up to 15 ms (walk_hard), 90 ms (static_hard, 11 gaps >20 ms; linearly interpolated => under-integrates). At 3476 dps ~17 deg/sample. Above ~2600 dps bursts under-integrate 25-40 %.
- C1 recommendation: R1-form with (i) state-age cap (~1.0 s, unverified), (ii) saturation flag (any raw axis within ~5 % of clip) that widens the allowance instead of the fitted 0.05*G*dt, (iii) wire headset imu0 gyro as ego-motion source. Never ship constant 45.
## C2 (statistics/generalisation) - reproduced A2 (83,279 GOOD/313 BAD state_ok; R1 0 FP/240; const 45 0 FP/232; const 30 4 FP/251; 7 GOOD>25 deg; LORO 41.9/25.2) and A1 crossings; NOT reproduced: shipped-rule counts, weak-confirm pair stats, wrong-state adjustment, live-vs-replay validation.
- Honest FP bounds: pooled 0/83,279 (3.6e-5) is dominated by dt<=50 ms (81,850). Relevant stratum dt>=50 ms: 1,429 GOOD on 1,127 anchors, 184 ten-second blocks -> 95 % upper bound 2.1e-3 per candidate, 5.1e-3 per 2 s block, 1.6e-2 per 10 s block. GOOD with gyro>1500 & dt>=50 ms: 228 on 180 anchors, 4 recordings, 48 % walk_hard (anchor upper bound 1.7e-2). Between-pair log-normal predictive max GOOD innov: q90 29.7, q95 33.9, q99 44.4 deg => constant 45 has ~1 % chance per new controller/recording pair of a first FP.
- Nested CV (LORO, leave-controller-out, leave-both-hard, leave-walk_hard, leave-all-walks, leave-medium): margin 0-10 %: walk_hard 5 FP, right controller 5-8 FP, all walks 8-11 FP; margin 50 %: still 2 FP on walk_hard. CV picks b=0 or 0.02; b=0.05 supported only by 3-5 walk_hard-right anchors. Threshold by recording set: all 41.9; w/o walk_hard 25.2; static_hard alone 19.6. Walk_hard is the only tail driver.
- Headroom (thr / max GOOD innov) and catches: const 45 1.08 (232); const 50 1.19 (225); R1 a=35,b=.05 1.43 (240); a=40,b=.05 1.63 (230); a=45,b=.05 1.82 (226); a=40,b=.03,cap 75 1.56 (234). Uncapped R1 reaches 209 deg (never vetoes); cap ~75 costs nothing (with headset). C2 recommendation: a=40, b<=0.05, cap ~75 deg (headroom >= 1.6); if gyro term unproven: constant 50; never constant <=45. Blocker: b=0.05 fitted to 3-5 walk_hard-right anchors.
- Labels: changing label cutoff (15/20/30 deg; pos 60/100/none) barely moves FP/catch; position-only-bad 50 candidates never caught by rot rules (median innov 9 deg). 6 GOOD>25 deg are true gyro prediction failures at walk_hard fast motion; 4 state_ok GOOD have state error 24.6-25.0 (state-boundary artefacts). 1,664 candidates lack GT (excluded from FP and catch => FP=0 omits mocap-occluded stretches).
- Wrong state: 147 valid candidates from bad states; R1 flags 38/71 GOOD -> lock-out unless reject-streak escape exists (closed loop not simulated).
- Sparse regimes (stay conservative): dt>700 ms (27 BAD, 153 GOOD; only 20 GOOD > 1.5 s); dt>700 & gyro>1500 (2 BAD, 18 GOOD); gyro>2000 (741 samples: 425 walk_hard, 309 static_hard); dt>=50 ms & gyro>1500 (228 GOOD, 4 recordings); first-after-gap with wrong state (71 GOOD, 56 anchors).
- POSITION: evidence does NOT support loosening position budgets (position innov useless beyond 50 ms; every tightened pos rule adds 4-5 catches for 13-17 FP). Policy p90<=50 mm pooled crossings by accel bin (<2/2-5/5-10/10-20/20-40/40-80) ~200/150/100/75/35/22 ms vs shipped 66-1.145 ms*A floor 35: shipped too tight at low accel, too loose above ~40 m/s2 (35 ms floor admits p90 58-82 mm); LORO fails on hard recordings for any looser fit; walk_medium/static_medium 2-4x worse than dark/easy; depends on dev-only mocap headset (no-mocap +30 ms p50 at 0.25 s). Keep 66 ms/0.001145 for accel<~20; consider tightening floor above ~40 m/s2.
- Evidence needed before loosening anything: >=15 recordings incl 3+ independent walk_hard-like sessions, >=20 GOOD anchors per (dt, gyro) bin, a no-mocap replay.
